How Bầu Cua Works

Bầu Cua Tôm Cá is a traditional Vietnamese dice game played during Lunar New Year. Three dice are rolled, each showing one of six symbols: Gourd (Bầu), Crab (Cua), Fish (Cá), Rooster (Gà), Shrimp (Tôm), and Deer (Nai).

Players bet on one or more symbols. For each die matching your bet, you win 1x your wager. If none match, you lose your wager. So a triple match pays 3:1!

House Edge: The expected value for a single-symbol bet is -7.87% per round (−17/216). This means for every $100 wagered, you expect to lose ~$7.87 on average. The game is always in favor of the house in the long run.

Strategies to Maximize Winnings

1. Bankroll Management (Most Important)

Set a budget and stick to it. Decide how much you're willing to lose before you start. Never chase losses. The best "strategy" is knowing when to walk away while ahead.

2. The Kelly Criterion

Since the expected value is negative, the true Kelly Criterion says don't bet at all. However, if you want to play for fun, a fractional Kelly approach (betting 1-2% of your bankroll per round) minimizes the risk of ruin while keeping you in the game longer.

3. Single Symbol vs. Multi Symbol

Betting on all 6 symbols equally guarantees a loss of 50% every round (you lose 3 bets and win 3 back at 1x minus the 3 losing bets).

Betting on 1-2 symbols gives higher variance — bigger wins and bigger losses — but the house edge per dollar wagered is the same (-7.87%). Fewer symbols = more volatility = more chances for a hot streak.

4. Quit While Ahead

Set a win target. If you start with $1,000 and your goal is to win $200, stop when you hit $1,200. Discipline is the best weapon against a negative-EV game.

Stop-loss: Similarly, if you've lost 30-50% of your bankroll, walk away and enjoy the festival!

5. Exploit Short-Term Variance

The fewer rounds you play, the more likely variance will work in your favor. Play a small number of rounds with larger bets (within your comfort zone) rather than many rounds with small bets — the house edge accumulates over time.
